How it works. Real return = (1 + return) ÷ (1 + inflation) − 1. Coast number = FIRE number ÷ (1 + real return)years to retirement. The coast line rises each year because fewer years remain to compound. Where your savings path crosses it, you can stop contributing and still reach your FIRE number at retirement. Today's money vs. future money. Enter spending at today's prices. The calculator compounds at the real return, so every figure and the chart stay in today's money. The FIRE tile also shows the same target in retirement-year prices for reference. Entering an already-inflated spending figure would count inflation twice. Keep in mind. Returns are smooth averages; real markets are not. A 3–3.5% withdrawal rate is a common conservative choice for early retirees. This is a planning aid, not financial advice.
