What it is

Most personal-finance content in Spain is either a lecture or a sales pitch. Historias de Pasta does it the other way round: every lesson is a short story about a character, with the real numbers behind it. Revolving cards, bank fund fees, margin calls, mortgages, compound interest, emergency funds.

The tagline of the most-watched series says it all: “No seas como Dani.”

Dani
Makes the mistakes so you don't have to: the revolving card, the expensive bank fund, the margin loan.
María
Dani's sister. Started investing ten years earlier, and compound interest did the rest.
Paco
The narrator and the explainer. When Dani gets into trouble, Paco steps in to break down the numbers.

How it's made

It's a side project next to my full-time job, so it only works as a system. Original, character-driven explainers made with AI, with every script fact-checked and approved by a human.

01 · Script
Story first, numbers checked
Each episode is a story with explainer pauses. Every figure is sourced and every script is approved by me before anything gets made.
02 · Voice & scenes
Consistent characters
Voices with ElevenLabs, cartoon scenes from saved characters with Higgsfield, so Dani always looks like Dani.
03 · Edit
The video motor
An automated pipeline assembles full 15–20 minute episodes, and each Short series has its own template built in HyperFrames.
04 · Publish & learn
Four platforms, one loop
Scheduling and analytics through Metricool. The same numbers feed the dashboard above and the next round of scripts.
